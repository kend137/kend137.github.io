About Me

Creation Is My Passion

Growing up I always knew I wanted to create something everyone could utilize and enjoy. From a very young age I was introduced to a game where I could create anything I wanted with code. From that point on my love for coding only grew. I graduated Rowan University with a degree in computer science honing in on my skills as a programmer and led a small group of developers on Roblox throughout my years at college which would not have been possible without my footing in Boy Scouts as an Eagle Scout and esports captain for my Universities Valorant Team. I hope I get the chance to tell you more about myself so please do not be afraid to contact me!

Highlights

  • Eagle Scout - Completely reconstructed the fence at my elementary schools parking lot to ensure the safety of kids walking around the area.
  • Roblox Game Developer - Created and leads a team of 3D artists, sound designers, and animators to produce games for everyone to enjoy.
  • Rowan University Valorant Esports Team Captain - Led my schools team through online and in person tournaments.
